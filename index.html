FROM RELIGIOSITY TO SPIRITUALITY

Shri Pundrik Goswami Ji Maharaj

“The whole world was looking at me, they were judging me. Is he capable of sitting on the seat? Will he be able to do anything? He is too young!”

— Maharaj Shri Pundrik Ji, TEDx Stage

In 2017, through the prestigious global stage of TEDx, Maharaj Shri Pundrik Goswami Ji shared the defining turning point of his life in a talk titled "From Religiosity to Spirituality". Today, as a devoted Seva Adhikari of Shri Radha Raman Ji, he travels worldwide to awaken Krishna consciousness and unconditional love for God.

His philosophy harmonizes eternal Vedic scriptures with modern scientific logic, empowering young minds to overcome anxiety, negativity, and material confusion through awakened inner consciousness.

CHARITABLE PILLARS

Vaijayanti Sewa Sansthan Trust

Established under the guidance of Pujya Maharaj Ji to serve humanity, protect Sanatan Dharma, shelter cows, and nurture Vedic values.

Gaushala Sewa

Protecting indigenous Desi cows with nutritious green fodder, 24/7 veterinary care, and loving shelter at Vrindavan & Punjab gaushalas.

Daily Annadanam

Serving thousands of warm, pure satvik prasadam meals daily to visiting pilgrims, sadhus, widows, and needy families across Vraj.

Vedic Gurukul & Education

Providing free traditional Sanskrit education, Vedas, Bhagavad Gita lessons, and modern IT literacy through Nimai Pathshala.

Medical Relief Camps

Organizing free eye surgeries, diagnostic health camps, and distribution of medicines for impoverished elderly devotees in Vrindavan.
